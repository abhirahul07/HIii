Part I

The story we could have lived.

Just imagination. None of this actually happened — but if it had, I have a feeling we would have been pretty good at being annoying little best friends.

At 5 or 6

We’d probably be together all day.

You’d be the little troublemaker I somehow kept ending up with… and I’d pretend I was the responsible one.

Best friends, obviously

We’d play, fight, eat…

We’d fight over the smallest things, get dramatic for five minutes, and somehow be completely fine again before anyone noticed.

Our roles were obvious

I’d be the innocent one. You’d be the warrior.

I’d just stand there looking harmless while you fought every tiny battle for both of us — toys, snacks, arguments… probably even my battles. 😌😂

And yes…

I’d spend half my day annoying you.

You’d spend the other half pretending you weren’t enjoying it. 😄

Then came school

Same class. Same section.

We’d share notes, help each other study, and somehow make even ordinary school days feel like an adventure.

The little things

You’d get me into things I’d never try alone.

You’d convince me to bunk a class, make ridiculous excuses with me, and somehow I’d still end up being the one protecting you from teachers. 😂

And maybe…

something would have started changing.

We’d still call it friendship. We’d still pretend nothing was happening. But somewhere in those school days, maybe the story would have started feeling a little different.

If that version had happened…

Maybe you’d have
proposed first. 😌

At least that’s the story I would have told you just to annoy you.

But the real story is better.
Part II • What actually happened

Then we met.
For real.

No imaginary classrooms. No made-up childhood. Just two people, a new city, a few adventures… and something that slowly became impossible to ignore.

The beginning

It started with little adventures.

Two friends having fun, talking too much, and slowly becoming a bigger part of each other’s everyday life.

Imagica

More places. More memories.

Some days were just about going somewhere. Some days were about the conversations on the way there.

A little dressed up

Still friends… right?

At least that’s what we kept telling ourselves.

Somewhere along the way

I started falling for you.

Not all at once. Just a little more with every trip, every conversation, every ordinary moment that stopped feeling ordinary.

Rajasthan

We kept collecting memories.

New places. Long conversations. And a feeling that kept quietly growing.

More of us

And somehow, it felt easy.

Like being around you had become one of my favourite things without either of us officially saying it.

December

Something about this trip felt different.

Maybe I was imagining it. Maybe you were starting to feel it too.

Mumbai

By then, I didn’t need an excuse to see you.

I just wanted to spend time with you. That was becoming reason enough.

Bangalore

Then came Nandi Hills.

A little ride, a little view, a lot of smiles… and a feeling I couldn’t keep pretending wasn’t there.

More little moments

The kind you don’t plan.

The kind that quietly become the memories you want to keep forever.

And then…

I knew I had to say it.

I had thought about this moment more times than I should admit.

The moment I finally stopped overthinking

And then… I asked you.

No imaginary version this time. Just me, standing there, finally saying what I had been feeling.

After that

Everything felt a little more real.

And honestly, I think the smiles said enough.

Us, after that

No more “what ifs”.

Just us, collecting the kind of little moments that make a story feel like home.

A few more smiles

And a lot more reasons to laugh.

Some memories are quiet. Some are silly. I want all of them.

Still us

Still making faces.

Still annoying each other. Still choosing each other.

The little things

These are the moments I’ll remember.

Not because they were perfect — because they were ours.

And finally

Just us.

Exactly where I want this story to keep going.

From imagination to reality

From friends
to forever. ❤️

Maybe our story didn’t begin when we were five. Maybe you didn’t actually propose to me first. 😌

But somehow, we found our way here — and I wouldn’t trade the real version for anything.

Happy Birthday, my love.
Here’s to all the memories we haven’t made yet.
