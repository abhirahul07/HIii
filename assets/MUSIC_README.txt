Music uses the official YouTube videos for Yaaron (KK) and Dooron Dooron (Paresh Pahuja).
The website starts Yaaron from the user's Begin click, then switches to Dooron Dooron when Part 2 enters view.
If a browser blocks autoplay, the visible music panel has normal YouTube controls; tap Play once.
Internet is required for the songs because the website does not bundle copyrighted audio files.
