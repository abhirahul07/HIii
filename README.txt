OUR LITTLE STORY — BIRTHDAY WEBSITE

Open index.html in a browser, or upload the entire folder to any static hosting service.

The website is designed as a responsive story experience rather than a photo album.
- Imaginary childhood/school section uses the imagined scenes.
- Real-life section uses the real photos.
- The proposal image is the exact photo supplied in the latest chat upload.
- Rotated real photos have been corrected in the assets.
- After-meeting photos are kept as real photos; there are no generated/animated replacements.
- Music buttons use official YouTube videos: Yaaron by KK and Dooron Dooron by Paresh Pahuja.
